How to use the shortlist

Use the shortlist to compare tutors against a student's request and record a coordinator decision.

  1. Choose a student. Select a student at the top of the Student request panel to load their saved dummy data.
  2. Review the request. Adjust the subject, level, curriculum, timezone, budget, language, or style preference as needed.
  3. Set ranking weights. Use the sliders to change how passing tutors are ranked across track record, style, scheduling, and subject depth.
  4. Find matches. Select Find matches to apply the hard filters and refresh the ranked shortlist.
  5. Review a tutor. Select a passing tutor to expand their score breakdown, then choose Propose to family or Skip.

Hard filters always apply before ranking. Every proposal or skip is recorded in the Decision log.

Decision log

Every action a coordinator takes on a shortlist is recorded here, so a match can be reviewed or handed off later.

This prototype demonstrates the intended split of responsibility: hard filters and scoring run automatically, weights and final proposal decisions stay with the coordinator. In production, tutor data would come from the shared CRM, and track-record scores would update from the ongoing check-in process rather than being fixed values.
